ABOUT ME

I like the part where the answer isn't obvious yet.

My background combines business analytics with psychology, so I tend to look at problems from both sides: what does the data show, and what might explain the people behind it?

I enjoy coding most when it feels like a puzzle. I like troubleshooting, testing an idea and figuring out how to get from an imperfect dataset to an answer someone can actually use. Psychology adds research design, behavior, ethics and comfort with findings that are not neat or expected.

That combination has taken me from People Analytics and machine learning to occupational fraud research, animal welfare data, AI and organizational psychology.
