ABOUT ME

I like the part where the answer isn't obvious yet.

My background combines business analytics with psychology, which means I tend to look at problems from both sides: what does the data show, and what might explain the people behind it?

I discovered that I enjoy coding most when it feels like a puzzle—troubleshooting, testing an idea, and figuring out how to get from an imperfect dataset to an answer someone can actually use. Psychology adds another layer: research design, behavior, ethics, and comfort with findings that aren't neat or expected.

That combination has taken me from people analytics and machine learning to occupational-fraud research, animal-welfare data, AI, and organizational psychology.
